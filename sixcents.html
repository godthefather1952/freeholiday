The split is chronological, not randomized. The model is fitted only on the first 70% and then frozen before the last 30% is evaluated.

No-look-ahead design

For historical window i, the locked price is that 15-minute candle's open and every indicator is calculated only from candles that ended before window i began. The candle's close is used only afterward as the Higher/Lower outcome. The 70/30 split occurs on a clean quarter-hour boundary, and the 30% verification segment never participates in calibration.
